If \(\sigma \) is a Primcodable type and \(f : \sigma \to \mathrm{Option}\, \sigma \) is computable, then \(\mathrm{StateTransition.eval}\, f : \sigma \to \mathrm{Part}\, \sigma \) is partial recursive.

Lemma 1.3 Reachability as finite iteration
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • StateTransition.reaches_iff_exists_iterate

A configuration \(s'\) is reachable from \(s\) under \(f\) if and only if there exists \(n \in \mathbb {N}\) such that \(n\)-fold Kleisli iteration of \(f\) starting at \(s\) yields \(s'\).

Proof ▶

By induction on Relation.ReflTransGen in the forward direction and induction on \(n\) in the reverse direction.
